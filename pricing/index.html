Why tasks and not tokens

Per-token billing asks you to predict a unit you cannot count, for work you have not seen yet. A task is one step that produced something you can see, so you can count it.

What counts as 1 task?

A task is one engine or memory-build step that produces an output. Several AI calls inside one step still count as one task. Finding things is free; accepting one uses 1 task.

Is there a free version?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 and includes 300 tasks a month. It does not include CRM or History & memory. AI runs on your own free Google key, which needs no card.

What happens when my free Google allowance runs out?

The job keeps going. Your own key is tried first, always. If it has been refusing for about 90 seconds and you are signed in, engine work is carried on our connection instead so a run finishes rather than dying halfway.

One thing this cannot rescue

Steps that drive a real browser, like studying your brand or reading a competitor's pages, cannot be carried that way. Those stop and say so rather than guessing at an answer. We would rather tell you here than have you discover it mid-run.

What happens when I use up my tasks?

Beyond your plan's allowance, extra tasks cost $0.03 each, paid from task credits you can buy. When your allowance and credits run out, the app stops and asks you to upgrade. It never runs a negative balance.

Are images and video included?

AI images and video renders are not counted in tasks on any plan. Each one is paid from media credits, and the price is shown before you render.

Approving a prompt is not a render

Approving an image prompt costs nothing. You render in the studio when you actually want the picture, and that is the moment media credits are used.

How do organisations work?

Only an Enterprise plan holder can create an organisation. Anyone, including Free users, can join one with an invite code. Members use the organisation's pooled tasks and features.

Can I run up a bill by accident?

No. It checks your remaining tasks and task credits before a step runs. When both are used up it stops and asks you to upgrade. Anything that spends money also needs your approval first.
