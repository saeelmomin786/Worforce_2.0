It is a fixed price you saw first

A flat fee for a finished job, shown before the work starts. Ten cents for a lead you decide to keep. One euro for a tailored application. The same number every time, not a token count you have to reverse-engineer afterwards.

the number on the button

Why this matters more than the rate

Per-token billing asks you to predict a unit you cannot count, for work you have not seen yet. That is what stops people using AI properly: not the price, the uncertainty. Here you always know which of the two states you are in before anything runs.

Is there a free version?

The app is free to download, and the everyday work is free to run. It uses your own free Google key, and it picks a cheap AI for simple steps and a strong one only where the job needs it. You pay when an engine finishes something chargeable.

What happens when my free allowance runs out?

The job keeps going. Your own key is tried first, always. If it has been refusing for about 90 seconds and you are signed in, engine work is carried on our connection instead so a run finishes rather than dying halfway.

One thing this cannot rescue

Steps that drive a real browser, like studying your brand or reading a competitor's pages, cannot be carried that way. Those stop and say so rather than guessing at an answer. We would rather tell you here than have you discover it mid-run.

Why isn't the video studio on a subscription?

Because almost all of its cost is per-second rendering that we get billed for by someone else. A flat monthly fee would be a promise we could not fund, so we do not make it.

The same applies to pictures and video inside marketing

Those are charged each time you render one, on every plan, including the €249 one. Approving an image prompt costs nothing. You render in the studio when you actually want the picture, and that is the moment it is charged.

We would rather put this on the pricing page than in a footnote you find later.

Can I run up a bill by accident?

No. It checks your balance before starting a chargeable job rather than after, and it stops at zero instead of running a negative balance. Anything that spends money also needs your approval first.
