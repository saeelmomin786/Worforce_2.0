Build your own

Write the steps in English. It builds the thing.

The twenty built-in engines cover the common jobs. Everything else, you can describe.

How do I make my own?

Type a name and the steps in ordinary words. It turns that into a working sequence of two to six steps, picking who does what and which connected apps each step needs.

  • It compiles in the background and retries on its own if something goes wrong.
  • Any step that sends something outside the company is forced to ask you first, unless you explicitly say otherwise. You cannot accidentally build something that emails people unattended.
  • Once built it behaves like any other engine: same task board, same approvals, same pricing.

Commissions

Can you build one for us?

Yes. Seven questions in plain English, each with a note explaining why we ask, plus your brand guidelines and up to three examples of work you would be happy to sign your name to.

The examples matter more than the brief

A brief tells us what you want. Three pieces of real work tell us what "good" means in your business, which is the thing that actually cannot be written down. That is how the engine learns your standard rather than a generic one.

What it costs

$500 to $2,000, plus $100 per extra piece. The exact number is set after a call, once we know what is actually involved.

How it arrives

A one-time install password. It then runs inside your copy like any other engine, on your machine, under your approvals.

What else can I set up myself?

Four things, none of which need anyone technical.

Saved routines

Multi-step jobs you have done once and want to run again the same way.

Dashboards

Upload a spreadsheet and it picks the charts and builds the dashboard. Or just ask the console to track something. It updates when you ask it to, and on its regular check.

Scheduled jobs

Recurring or one-off work that runs on its own: daily follow-ups, weekly reports, reminders.

The engine marketplace

A catalogue of engines your organisation can subscribe to, controlled by the owner.
