A few good questions.

Do I need to know how to use AI?+

No. Describe what you need in ordinary words. Connect the apps you choose and describe the work. Workforce handles the AI behind each task.

Will it send emails without me?+

Outbound actions require approval by default. If you do not respond within 90 seconds, the action stops. You can choose to disable approval for individual steps.

Is everything free?+

The Free plan includes 300 tasks each month. Images and video use separate media credits.

Does it work on my computer?+

Workforce is available for Windows, Linux (Intel/AMD) and Android. Mac and iPhone downloads are not available yet.
