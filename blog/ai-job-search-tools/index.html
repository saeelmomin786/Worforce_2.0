Reduce the dead ends first

Published 30 September 2026 by the Workforce team. An expired listing can look surprisingly current. Workforce opens posting pages and checks for redirects, closed applications, missing descriptions and other signs that the link is not a useful opportunity.

Set a realistic search direction

Begin with your actual CV and the roles you want to pursue. A short list that matches your experience is more useful than a large collection of loosely related titles. Review the suggested roles before accepting them into the application workflow.

Understand what a live check can do

The engine checks ten failure cases, including page errors, generic careers pages, sign-in pages and job lists mistaken for individual openings. This reduces obvious dead ends. A live page cannot guarantee that a hiring team will respond or that the role has not changed since the check.

Tailor the evidence, not the facts

A useful CV highlights the parts of your experience most relevant to the role. Workforce checks drafted claims against your source CV and prepares a tailored CV and cover letter. Review dates, qualifications and achievements carefully. Do not accept wording that gives a misleading impression of your experience.

Make your edits before exporting

Edit the generated text directly and rebuild the PDF from your version. Word files are supported as source CVs, but the app does not export Word documents. If an employer requires .docx, you will need another way to prepare that file.

Submit the application yourself

Workforce prepares the documents; you send them through the posting. This final check gives you a chance to confirm the employer, role, requested documents and application terms. The tool does not guarantee applicant-tracking success, interviews or an offer.

Keep cost and privacy clear

Finding postings is free. Accepting a posting and each document written use one task. Fifteen postings with a CV and letter each use 45 tasks. Your CV is stored and indexed locally, while online AI requests are governed by the relevant provider’s terms.
