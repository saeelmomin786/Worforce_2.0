Start with the work, not the job title

Published 26 September 2026 by the Workforce team. An AI marketing engineer connects tools and processes that help a campaign run. For a small business, the useful question is simpler: which repeated jobs could software prepare so your team can focus on decisions? How to Automate Business Processes Without Losing Your Data Privacy

Give the system something specific to learn

Start with your site, published posts, brand guidelines and examples you would happily approve. Workforce’s marketing engine uses this material to understand your business, then researches competitors on the platforms you use. Better source material gives you a better starting draft.

Turn the research into a calendar

The engine proposes a platform-specific plan and writes posts with suggested media. Review the calendar as a whole: does it address customer questions, reflect your priorities and sound like your business? Edit the wording before approving the slots you want to publish.

Connect the tools that carry the work

Gmail, LinkedIn and HubSpot are among the available connections, alongside Google and other work services. A connection only helps if it has a clear purpose in your process. Grant the access needed for the job and leave unrelated accounts disconnected.

Keep publishing a deliberate choice

Marketing supports Facebook, Instagram, Threads and LinkedIn. Approved slots enter the publishing queue. Approval is enabled by default, and you can control the setting per step. AI preparation does not remove the need to check facts, images and the timing of a campaign.

Measure useful enquiries

The engine can compare channels through leads per post, using its lead form and post-publication checks. Look for patterns that inform your next decision rather than treating a high engagement count as proof of business impact. Each post draft and competitor check uses one task; media renders have separate costs.

Keep the human judgment

You do not need to write code to use the ready-made workflow. You do need to decide what your business should say, which opportunities matter and what quality you will accept. Use the engine to reduce repeatable work while retaining that responsibility.
