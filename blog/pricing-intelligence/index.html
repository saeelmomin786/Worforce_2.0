Keep up with what competitors charge.

By Workforce 2.0 team · October 18, 2026

To keep up with what competitors charge, you need a system that captures live data before your rivals adjust their market positioning. Manually scouring websites wastes time and misses the moment a competitor shifts their strategy, but Workforce 2.0 automates the entire surveillance process to keep your pricing strategy ahead of the curve.

Read the source page

Automated monitoring is only useful if it pulls from the actual source of truth rather than cached or stale indexes. The engine extracts currency, plan names, features, and billing periods directly from the page text, ensuring you see exactly what your customers see.

Funding announcements and interface labels are filtered out so you only receive clean, actionable data. If the engine encounters a difficult layout, it may ask AI for help, but it never invents a number; a stored price must appear in the source text to be recorded.

Compare prices carefully

A market baseline becomes useless if you compare apples to oranges, so the system maintains strict distinctions between billing cycles. Monthly and yearly periods remain separate to ensure you calculate your relative cost accurately.

Prices in different currencies are flagged rather than silently converted, preventing errors from fluctuating exchange rates. If a business switches to sales-call-only pricing, the system marks the item as having no public price instead of generating an inaccurate estimate.

Get a recommendation with its working

Meaningful strategy advice relies on objective arithmetic rather than subjective AI sentiment. When you input your own price, the engine compares it against the market baseline to determine your relative positioning.

The system only offers a recommendation if it has at least three comparable plans, ensuring the advice is statistically sound. Results identify whether you should consider a price increase, review your position as a top-quarter provider, or maintain a mid-market rate.

Receive useful change alerts

Your pricing intelligence engine notifies you immediately when the competitive landscape shifts. Alerts distinguish between new promotions, price drops, plan removals, and competitors moving below your current threshold.

To avoid notification fatigue, the system only triggers an undercut alert when a competitor makes a new move below your price. It ignores static data, focusing your attention exclusively on the moments your market advantage is under threat.

Make room for your next big thing

Historical data reveals the difference between a one-off promotion and a permanent shift in market strategy. You stop guessing why a rival adjusted their rates and start identifying the patterns behind their behavior.

The engine keeps a repository of every change, allowing you to correlate pricing shifts with new features or seasonal cycles.

Frequently asked questions

Does the system use AI to guess competitor pricing?

No, Workforce 2.0 retrieves pricing data directly from web pages to ensure accuracy.

Can I track multiple competitors at once?

Yes, the engine tracks multiple competitors simultaneously to provide a comprehensive market baseline.

Is my data shared with other users?

No, all tracking and configuration happen locally on your own machine using Workforce 2.0.
