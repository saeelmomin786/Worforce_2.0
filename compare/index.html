When a chat assistant is enough

A chat assistant can be a good place to explore ideas, learn something or review a draft. Modern assistants vary in memory and connected tools. Choose based on what you need the software to actually do, rather than assuming every chatbot has the same limits.

When a workflow helps

Workforce organizes multi-step work around business jobs: research prospects, prepare outreach, review the messages and run the permitted next steps. The task board and recurring workflows help you track work beyond a single response.

Working alongside an agency

An agency can bring creative judgment, specialist expertise and an outside perspective. Workforce can help with repeatable research, drafting, scheduling and reporting. Compare the actual work you need and the agency’s proposal; there is no single price that represents every agency.

Working alongside your team

A person brings relationships, accountability and judgment. Workforce can prepare information and handle repeatable steps, while the responsible person reviews important decisions. Shared knowledge can make handovers easier but does not replace leadership or responsibility.

Compare the complete cost

Free begins at 300 monthly tasks. Basic is $19.99 a month, Pro $199.99 and Enterprise $19.99 per seat. Consider separate media credits and the time needed to review output. Completion time depends on the job, connected services and approvals.

Try a real job before deciding

Choose one recurring task you understand well. Run it with draft-only permissions, compare the result with your usual process and decide where it saves useful effort. Keep a person involved where errors would have legal, financial, employment or client consequences.
