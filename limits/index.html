Available devices and voices

Workforce runs on Windows 10 and 11, Linux and Android. Mac and iPhone releases are not available yet. Linux is x86_64 only. Read-aloud voices and voice cloning are Windows only, in English, French, German, Portuguese, Italian and Spanish.

Sales replies still need a person

The sales engine does not detect replies, calculate a reply rate or add opt-outs to the do-not-contact list automatically. You must record opt-outs yourself. The shared list is checked for outbound messages so a recorded opt-out can stop queued follow-ups.

Video is made scene by scene

Characters can remain consistent, but lips do not sync to spoken words. Clips are generated independently, so scenes cut rather than forming one continuous camera move. Export re-encodes on your machine and may take roughly as long as the film. Image and video renders always use media credits.

Approvals and documents

Marketing publishes approved calendar slots. Approval is enabled by default and can be changed per step. Job applications produce PDFs, not Word files; you submit the documents yourself. Review factual claims before publishing or sending any AI-generated work.

Plans and connectivity

Free excludes CRM and History & memory. Local reading, indexing and search can work offline; AI work cannot. AI requests go to online services. Optional team sharing uses your Drive. The entire product is not local-only.

Meetings and specialist engines

The meeting recorder listens through your microphone while you attend. It cannot join, speak or act in the meeting. IPO analysis is desktop only. SEO site studies, competitor reading and rank checks require the Windows or Linux browser. Informational analysis does not replace professional judgment.

Review important results

We select and connect the AI behind each task engine. You do not need to choose a model before starting work. AI output can still be wrong, so check important legal, financial, employment and client-facing work before relying on it.
