Start with the customers you want

Describe your ideal customer or use the customers you already have as a starting point. The engine searches with a real browser across search results and Google Maps, city by city. If a site requires a human check, it opens a window for you to complete it. Why AI Engine Market Share Doesn't Solve Your Workflow Problems

Review prospects before using tasks

Companies are checked against your criteria. The engine checks whether a contact domain accepts mail and filters generic addresses such as info@ and sales@. These checks reduce bad leads but do not guarantee a reply or prove every mailbox exists. Finding companies is free; accepting a lead uses one task.

Draft in your voice

The engine prepares outreach and learns from the difference between its drafts and what you actually send. Its own unedited autonomous messages are excluded from that learning. Import an existing list from a spreadsheet or paste it in; imported leads receive the same checks.

Send through your accounts

Use your connected mailbox or WhatsApp session. Phone numbers are formatted for WhatsApp without requiring you to save a contact first. Approve individual messages or a batch. Sending starts locked; you can change the engine’s approval setting. The engine checks for evidence that a WhatsApp message actually sent before reporting success.

Keep one do-not-contact list

The suppression list is shared across your products and team. An opt-out can cover a person’s email addresses and phone numbers, and is checked at send time, including for queued follow-ups. Entries do not expire automatically.

Handle replies yourself

Reply detection is not implemented. There is no automatic reply rate or automatic recording of opt-out requests. Review incoming replies and update the do-not-contact list yourself. Follow-up automation is not a substitute for that responsibility.

Count the work clearly

Finding is free. Accepting each lead, generating each draft and sending each message use one task apiece. A running campaign can top up its city-based prospect list every ten minutes. Sending pace and verification help manage outreach, but do not guarantee deliverability or protect a domain from every complaint.
