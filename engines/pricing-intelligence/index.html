Read the source page

The engine extracts currency, plan names, features and billing periods directly from page text. After three failed extraction attempts it may ask AI for help, but a stored price must still appear in the source. Funding announcements and interface labels are filtered out of the pricing data. Ensure data privacy in AI by keeping your operations local

Compare prices carefully

Monthly and yearly billing periods are kept distinct. Prices in different currencies are flagged rather than silently converted. A business with sales-call-only pricing is marked as having no public price rather than given an estimate.

Get a recommendation with its working

Recommendations use arithmetic rather than an AI opinion. Add your own price to compare with the market. With fewer than three comparable plans, the engine declines to recommend a change. Results can suggest considering a raise, reviewing a top-quarter position or staying with a mid-market price. Why You Should Stop Searching for an AI Engineer Market Rate

Receive useful change alerts

Alerts cover price rises, price drops, new plans, removed plans, promotions and competitors moving below your price. A promotion is flagged when it begins. An undercut alert is triggered by a new move below you, rather than repeated simply because a competitor remains cheaper.

Choose who matters

Suggested competitors enter an approve-or-reject queue. Rejected suggestions are remembered. The engine reads public pricing pages for products as well as software, provided usable prices are visible.

Know the frequency and cost

Finding competitors is free. Accepting a competitor and each competitor check use one task. Each rival is checked at most hourly, and each source page is read at most once a day. Pro includes unlimited tasks, but the reading frequency limits still apply.
