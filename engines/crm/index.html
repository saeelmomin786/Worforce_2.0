CRM

Your contacts are already in it. Nobody typed them in.

People and companies built from your inbox, your WhatsApp, your sales leads and your memory. Group them, send to them, automate the routine, and ask about any one of them.

On Basic, Pro and Enterprise. Its AI drafts, answers and summaries are never counted.

Where do the records come from?

From what Workforce 2.0 already holds. They are built fresh on every read and never copied, so a lead that changes in Sales is already changed here.

  • Sales leads become companies, with their phones, emails, stage and the Sales history for that lead.
  • Competitors from Marketing and SEO become companies, one per website.
  • The people you correspond with by email and WhatsApp become people. Machines, group chats and you are left out.
  • Person files in your memory become people, so everything said on every channel is one click away.
  • WhatsApp groups and labels you pick are imported without opening a chat, so nothing is marked as read.

Colleagues stay out

Your own addresses and your company's domain are always left out, and a never-add list blocks any address, number or domain you name.

Can I bring in my LinkedIn connections?

Yes, from LinkedIn's own export, Connections.csv. Each connection becomes a person with name, position, company and profile link, plus the email when they shared it.

  • People are grouped by role as they come in: founders and owners, CXOs, directors, sales, marketing, engineering and the rest.
  • A background job finds each company's website and reads its email, phone and description. It carries on after a restart.
  • There is no LinkedIn crawl. Only the file you export is read.

How do groups work?

A group is fixed members plus, if you like, everyone matching a saved filter, so it fills itself as records change.

  • Every Sales product is already a group, with its leads as members.
  • The same person in two groups is one recipient.
  • Views as a table, a kanban pipeline, a calendar or a list, with filters, sorting and saved views.

How does a campaign send?

By email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn, or one message per person on the first channel they can be reached on. You see every recipient's message, and who is left out and why, before anything goes.

  • Email goes out under the Sales engine's pacing and daily cap, from your connected mailbox.
  • WhatsApp waits 5 to 10 seconds between messages, rests after every 50, and stops at 400 a day counting Sales' own.
  • LinkedIn messages go from your own session to your first-degree connections, one page at a time.
  • Who gets it is decided again at send time, on the current records. A message caught mid-send by a restart is never sent twice.

What stops it messaging someone who opted out?

The same do-not-contact list the Sales engine uses. A record on it is blocked on every channel when the recipients are planned, and email and WhatsApp are checked again at the moment of sending.

Placeholder addresses are never sent to, and the Email and WhatsApp buttons are disabled on a do-not-contact record. How the Sales list works →

What can automations do?

Start when a record is created, updated or deleted, on a schedule, or by hand. Then update or create records, wait, add to a group, create a task, send an email or WhatsApp, ask AI, or call a web address.

  • Changes an automation makes never start automations, so two cannot loop.
  • At most 60 runs an hour.
  • A test run uses one real record and changes nothing and sends nothing.

What does its AI do?

Three things. Draft a short email or WhatsApp message from your business profile. Ask a question about one record. And with no question, summarise who they are, what has happened so far, and what to do next.

  • It answers from the record's fields, messages, notes and tasks, and says so when the record does not have the answer.
  • CRM AI is free and never counted, and it keeps running when your tasks are used up.

Where is it kept?

In a file on your computer. Turn sharing on and it syncs with your team through the hourly organisation sync, with each entry keeping its latest change.

Each member can be set to view or edit, and allowed or not to delete, import or send campaigns. A campaign only sends from the computer it was made on, so a team sync never sends it twice. How teams share →

What does it cost?

The CRM is part of the Basic, Pro and Enterprise plans, not the Free plan. CRM AI (drafts, ask, summaries) is free and never counted.

Questions

Can I import from another CRM?

Yes, from a CSV or Excel file, with presets for HubSpot, Pipedrive and Salesforce exports. You map the columns and choose what happens to rows that match records you already have.

Does it merge duplicates on its own?

No. It finds records that share an email, phone, domain or name and offers them to you. Merging is your call.

Does it read my Google Contacts?

No. It works the other way: an hourly sync writes CRM records into Google Contacts, under their own group.

Can the assistant send from the CRM?

No. The assistant can search, summarise, add records and draft a message, but sending stays in the CRM, with you.
