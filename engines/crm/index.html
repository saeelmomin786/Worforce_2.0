Build records from the work you do

Sales leads and approved competitors become companies. Email and WhatsApp correspondents and person files in memory become people. Records reflect the underlying data rather than separate stale copies. Your own addresses, company domain, machines and group chats are excluded; a never-add list gives you further control.

Import what you already have

Bring in LinkedIn’s Connections.csv export without crawling LinkedIn connections. Import CSV or Excel files from another CRM, with presets for HubSpot, Pipedrive and Salesforce. Map columns and choose how matching records are handled. Potential duplicates are suggested, but you decide whether to merge.

Organize people your way

Create groups with fixed members or saved filters that update automatically. Sales products already appear as groups. See records in tables, a kanban pipeline, a calendar or lists, with saved views. A person in more than one group still receives one campaign message.

Review recipients before a campaign

Prepare email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn outreach, or choose each person’s first available channel. Preview messages and exclusions before sending. The shared do-not-contact list applies. WhatsApp waits 5 to 10 seconds between messages, rests after 50 and has a combined Sales/CRM limit of 400 a day. LinkedIn messaging uses your session for first-degree connections.

Automate routine record work

Start an automation from a record change, a schedule or a manual action. It can update records, wait, add a group member, create tasks, draft work or perform allowed sends. Automation-made changes do not trigger other automations, preventing loops. There is a limit of 60 runs per hour; a test uses one real record without changing or sending anything.

Ask about a relationship

CRM AI can draft a short message, answer a question about a record or summarize the history and next step. Answers draw on the record’s fields, messages, notes and tasks. These AI drafts, questions and summaries are not counted as tasks. CRM itself requires Basic, Pro or Enterprise.

Share with clear permissions

CRM data is local and can join the hourly team sync. Set members’ view, edit, delete, import and campaign permissions. A campaign sends only from its originating computer. Google Contacts sync writes CRM records outward hourly; it does not import your Google Contacts. The assistant can search, summarize and draft, but campaign sending stays in the CRM.
