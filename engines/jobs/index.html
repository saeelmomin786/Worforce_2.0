Start from your actual experience

Upload your CV and describe the roles you want. Matching focuses on jobs your experience can reasonably support. The engine changes how that experience is presented for a role; it should not add qualifications or achievements you do not have.

Check that the listing is real and current

Every link is opened and checked for ten common failure cases: a missing site, page error, expired posting, help page, sign-in page, generic careers page, closed applications, missing job description, a list of jobs rather than one job, or an unrelated redirect.

Prepare documents for the role

The engine compares the job description with your source CV and drafts a tailored CV and cover letter. Claims are checked against the source text, and generic filler is removed. It can use the language expected by the posting.

Edit the version you will use

Make changes directly in the draft. The PDF is rebuilt from your edits, so the final document reflects your review. Word documents can be used as input, but the app does not export .docx.

You submit the application

Workforce prepares documents; it does not automatically apply for you. Check every claim and send the documents yourself through the verified posting. Live checks reduce wasted effort but cannot guarantee an interview or acceptance by an applicant tracking system.

Pay for useful output

Finding listings is free. Accepting a posting uses one task and each document written uses one task. Fifteen accepted postings with a CV and cover letter each use 45 tasks, within the Free plan’s 300 monthly tasks.
