Study your site and competitors

The engine crawls up to 1,000 pages, using the sitemap and links while respecting robots.txt. It records titles, descriptions, headings, links and raw HTML, and looks for pages with no incoming links. Index checks use Google and Bing where available. You approve or remove suggested competitors.

Choose topics with a reason

Topics come from competitor gaps, rising searches and requests from Marketing and News. A site must appear in at least two of your searches to qualify as a search-discovered competitor. If you already rank for a topic, the engine can recommend refreshing the existing page. Bing search counts and Google suggestion counts are labelled separately.

Bring something original to the draft

Drafting needs evidence only your business can supply, such as a case study, internal data, a customer insight, an expert quote or a first-hand opinion. Figures must be supported by cited source text. Links outside your site and provided sources are removed, and unresolved checks are shown for review.

Confirm the claims before publishing

The review lists brand-name sentences, list items, FAQ answers and the image for confirmation. Editing a confirmed claim or changing the image resets that confirmation. Publish remains disabled until required checks pass. The detector can miss claims that do not name your brand, so read the whole draft.

Publish through your own site connection

For GitHub, the engine creates a commit on a new seo/ branch and opens a pull request. Publish attempts to merge it; branch protection or conflicts leave it open for a person. For WordPress, it creates a draft that you publish in WordPress. Supporting index, sitemap and relevant internal links accompany the article.

Measure and maintain

The engine checks the live article and measures Search Console results at days 3, 7, 14 and 30, then monthly. It compares two 28-day windows weekly for declining pages. The competitor monitor can read up to eight approved competitor blogs daily and check new-post rankings weekly for 30 days. Missing data is labelled unmeasured. Turning the engine off pauses scheduled runs, while manual buttons still work.

Costs and practical limits

Each finished job uses one task. Competitor jobs use one task per kept competitor; keeping a discovered competitor costs one task, while one recommended by Marketing is free. AI images use media credits; your images and suitable attributed Wikimedia images are preferred. Browser studies and rank checks need Windows or Linux. There is no backlink, domain-authority, keyword-density or target-word-count scoring.
