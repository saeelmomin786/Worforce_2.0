Read without changing your unread markers

Choose the Gmail mailboxes the engine can read. It leaves messages unread until you read them, preserving your existing signal for what you have seen. WhatsApp and selected social pages can work alongside the mailbox.

Prepare a reply in your voice

The engine learns openings, sign-offs and tone from past correspondence. The writing profile is an editable text file and is refined over time. You can change or discard a draft before it is sent.

Keep mailboxes separate

Connect several Google accounts at once and assign each job to a specific account. Writing profiles are per mailbox, so a reply for someone whose diary you manage can sound like them. The engine does not file or archive their mail as part of reading it.

Bring buried requests into view

Requests inside long email threads are pulled into a list, helping you catch work that might otherwise be missed. You decide which mailboxes and groups are available and can change that selection.

Review before sending

Drafts wait for your approval. If an approval request is unanswered, the send is refused. This gives an assistant a faster first draft while leaving control over the final message with the person responsible.

What it costs and supports

Reading is free. Each drafted reply and each sent message uses one task. Pro includes unlimited tasks. Gmail is the supported email mailbox today; do not assume Outlook mailbox support from the wider connected-app catalogue.
