Google tools

The 36-connector catalogue includes Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Sheets, Docs, Slides, Maps and YouTube, plus an in-house Google tools server. Google Ads and Analytics are also accessible through Google services. The source lists 33 Gmail tools, 35 Drive tools, 25 Calendar tools and 15 Sheets tools. Work and customer apps

Connect GitHub, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, LinkedIn, Airtable, Supabase and SQLite. LinkedIn has 18 listed tools. Available actions depend on the service connection and the permissions you grant.

Finance and research

Finance connections include Stripe, PayPal, Xero, SEC EDGAR and Yahoo Finance. Research tools include Brave Search, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, arXiv, Hacker News, YouTube transcripts, Firecrawl and Bright Data.

Local tools and WhatsApp

The catalogue also includes your filesystem, memory, structured thinking and time. WhatsApp uses an embedded session shared by the sales and inbox workflows. Account-free tools are ready immediately; services needing an account require you to sign in.

Bring your existing setup

Import standard connector configuration from Claude Desktop, Cursor, VS Code or Windsurf. You can add compatible connectors beyond the built-in list. Remote connections must be secure unless they run on your own machine.

Keep accounts and permissions separate

Several Google accounts can be connected simultaneously, with each job pinned to one account. Worker permissions limit which tools each worker may reach. Connect the services you need and keep unrelated accounts out of the workflow.

Optional desktop access

Workforce can act on the desktop and inspect installed applications if you enable that capability. It is off by default and uses the same approval controls. Connected services may have their own pricing, access requirements and limits.
